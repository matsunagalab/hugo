T. Murakami, Y. Hashidate, and Y. Matsunaga published a paper in Bioinformatics.

We fine-tuned the protein language model ESM-2 on nanobody thermostability data and used sparse autoencoders (SAEs) to decompose its embeddings into sparse, interpretable features. The analysis identified known determinants of stability, such as the VHH tetrad and disulfide bonds, as well as candidate stabilizing residues, which were supported by free energy perturbation calculations.
